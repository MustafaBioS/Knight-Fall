# Medieval Spear – Stylized 3D Asset

A stylized medieval spear asset for RPGs, NPCs, or weapon systems.

## Features:
- Formats: FBX / OBJ / GLB
- PBR textures (Base Color, Metallic, Roughness, Normal)
- Lightweight, low-poly, and UV-unwrapped
- Game-engine ready

## How to Use:
1. Import into your 3D software or engine (Unity, Unreal, etc).
2. Drag the model into the scene or attach to a character rig.
3. Apply textures and adjust materials as needed.
